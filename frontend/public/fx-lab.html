Shake 4 ready

Tier is derived, not assigned. Each chip carries a real type, rank and card count, and the page runs the same tierOf() and labelFor() that run in game, so a chip can never claim a word the rules would not produce. All three values already come from the server on lastPlay, so shipping this needs no backend change.

Shake is a seven-step ladder. Drag the slider and hit Feel it to compare any two rungs back to back; Force pins every play to the slider value so you can judge one strength against the whole banner. The px and ms readout is the real spec — GameBoard.tsx runs the same seven numbers through framer-motion that this page runs through CSS.

The banner sits on top of a real play. Every fire deals the cards into the hand and flies them out of it — 420ms each, 45ms apart, landing 80% of the way in, arcing over the line and squashing on contact, with dust thrown out from under each one. Those are cardFlight.ts's four numbers, and the table takes its first knock the frame the card lands, exactly as GameBoard.tsx schedules it. Switch the seat to 左家 or 右家 and the cards launch from that chair instead, the way an opponent's do — they are only ever a face-down count, so the seat is the only real origin.

終局模式 plays the whole ending. The winning play empties the hand and game_over rides in with it, which is why the result screen has to wait: it goes up only once the banner, the cold open and the finale are finished. The room resets on the server's own clock twelve seconds later — watch return_to_lobby land on the wire and the table go back to a lobby underneath a result screen that is a snapshot and outlives it. Dismissing is the player's call, not the server's, so a 46-second track can finish.

The video finales are endings too. 黑棺 and 閻魔刀 are the two ways a round can be taken outright, and both play here the way they play in game, on whichever chip you fired last. 黑棺 holds the table through 鏡花水月, lands the winning cards when the line ends, runs its plate, and closes the shutter — the result screen mounts behind the shut leaves and is revealed by them opening. 閻魔刀 has no line: the cards go down with the first frame, and the result screen goes up over the clip at 11s, one second before the room resets, with the clip playing straight through both until it is dismissed. 無量空処 has no place in the game yet, so it stays what it is here — a plate preview, no cards and no result. All three are keyed rather than composited: the black, the white flash and the green screen are dropped by the SVG filters above, which is why the felt shows through them.
